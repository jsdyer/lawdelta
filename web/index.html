Privacy law watchlist

Two Acts. Two regulations. A deliberately small, complete-text collection.

Capture dates are observation dates

A diff says the published text changed between two fetches. It does not establish the day a law came into force. The first capture is a baseline and has no invented earlier version. Repeated unchanged captures are useful evidence too.

Sections use their printed numbers; schedules are compared as whole units. XML formatting and whitespace are normalized. This is a limited watchlist, not the full BC statute book.

Effective-date annotations & privacy

Regulation amendment dates are copied only from official XML metadata and shown separately from observed text changes. Pending dates remain pending; a document-level date is not assigned to a section. Acts may have no such annotations.

Your filters stay in this tab. The browser reads static captures only. A weekly collector reads four official CiviX document endpoints and the documented search API for catalogue metadata.
